### AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship | Week 5

> Turning customer data into actionable retention strategies.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

For Week 5 of my AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship, I worked on a Business Analytics Case Study using the **Telco Customer Churn dataset**.

The goal was to understand **why customers leave a telecom company, identify the customer segments most likely to churn, and provide data-driven recommendations that could improve customer retention.**

This project helped me move beyond simply analyzing data to thinking about the **business decisions behind the numbers.**

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The company is experiencing customer churn and needs to understand:

- Why are customers leaving?
- Which customer groups are most likely to churn?
- What factors are associated with higher churn?
- What actions can the business take to improve retention?

---

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains **7,043 customer records** covering:

- Customer demographics
- Tenure
- Contract type
- Internet services
- Technical support
- Payment methods
- Monthly charges
- Total charges
- Customer churn

## 📊 Key Findings

### 1. Contract Type

Month-to-month customers had the highest churn rate.

| Contract | Churn Rate |
|---|---:|
| Month-to-month | **42.7%** |
| One year | **11.3%** |
| Two year | **2.8%** |

This suggests that customers with lower contractual commitment are significantly more likely to leave.

### 2. Customer Tenure

Churned customers had an average tenure of approximately **18 months**, compared with about **38 months** for customers who stayed.

This highlights the importance of retaining customers during the early stages of their relationship with the company.

### 3. Monthly Charges

Customers who churned had higher average monthly charges:

- Churned: **74.44**
- Retained: **61.27**

### 4. Payment Method

Customers using **electronic checks** recorded the highest churn rate among the payment methods analyzed, at approximately **45.3%**.

### 5. Internet Service

Fiber optic customers recorded a significantly higher churn rate than DSL and customers without internet service.

This should be treated as an area for further investigation rather than proof that fiber service itself causes churn.

---

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis shows that churn is not evenly distributed across the customer base.

The highest-risk patterns were associated with:

- Month-to-month contracts
- Shorter customer tenure
- Higher monthly charges
- Electronic-check payments
- Limited support services

These patterns provide useful signals for targeted customer retention.

---

## 🚀 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, I recommend that the business:

1. **Encourage longer-term contracts** through discounts, loyalty benefits or additional services.

2. **Strengthen early customer engagement** by introducing onboarding and check-in programs for newer customers.

3. **Investigate the fiber optic customer experience**, including pricing, reliability and customer support.

4. **Review value for high-charge customers** through personalized plans and loyalty offers.

5. **Develop targeted retention campaigns** using multiple customer risk indicators instead of treating all customers the same.
